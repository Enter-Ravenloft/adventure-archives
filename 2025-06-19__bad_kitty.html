DM [Esh] APP 19-06-2025 09:28 PM
Whispers are nothing new. Whispers through the taverns and the alleyways. Whispers amongst the laborers and the travelers. The people of Barovia are always saying things under hushed breath because the dangers of Barovia are always ever present and you better whisper, lest those dangers hear you. You catch snippets of these whispers, and though not unusual for either party a specific word catches your attention. Cats. The Barovian people are a suspicious lot and so a mention here or there wouldn't be suspicious on its own either but today these whispers are not the usual black cats and voodoo dogs. The locals are complaining about meowing all through the night but they don't just hear it, it's as if they feel it. Through their bones. As if a massive cat is purring... or possibly hundreds in unison. And though whatever is going on has been quite helpful for any rodent problems the newer problems are so much worse. Damage to people and to the property everywhere. Reports and witnesses finding cat like creatures that are as big as adult!! Brave adventures, renowned for your expertise and heroics have been asked to please investigate. Reaching the outskirts of the city you find yourself amongst other heroes and adventures where you spend a moment greeting each other and preparing for the unknown.
