Beyond the bounds of Vallaki - past the last lantern-lit fences and the uneasy comfort of the town’s walls - the Old Svalich Road road gives way to wild earth, where trees grow sparse and crooked and their branches claw toward the sky like grasping fingers. Adventurers in Barovia call these unpopulated areas simply "The Wilds," and in one such place - not far from the Vistani camp - a low rise of land breaks through the brush. A small gathering has formed - figures drawn by curiosity, rumor, or just personal amusement at the passions and theories of a seafaring druidic halfling. Nearby, a kobold works intently over an assortment of lenses and brass fittings, assembling and adjusting some kind of viewing device with an energetic spirit. The discussion circles around a single, unsettling idea: that something in the sky is not behaving as it should. Above, a cluster of ravens crowd the branches of the surrounding trees. They do not caw or scatter, but simply watch as though following the conversation. The sky itself is thick with layered cloud, and it's clear the group is hopeful for a clearing that might make real, what is now only discussion. And those gathered on the hill - whether scholars, skeptics, or wanderers drawn by chance - find themselves with the same question pressing in: Is it something trying to reach us here in this dark prison?
