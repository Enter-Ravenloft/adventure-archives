The Two Wakes (Vallaki Edition)

The fog has been with you since the you passed the edge of the village on the way to this homestead on its outskirts, and it only thickens as the cabin comes into view,. On floor and seemingly small, its windows are dark but one. Off to the left slumps a detached shed, its door hanging crooked. Between it and the cabin, an old well sits under a plank cover, the stones around it are covered with wet moss. Radu waits for you on the porch with a lantern in one old crooked hand. She doesn't waste words, and leads you inside. The cabin is bigger than it looked. A kitchen on the far side of the main room with a cold basin, cold stove, and herbs gone brittle on their hooks above. The hearth room holds the only warmth in the house, but the small fire seems to throw more shadow than light. Past them are two small bedrooms. In the nearer one, laid out on the bed beneath a grey sheet, hands folded, a coin resting on each closed eye, is Katarina Nurbek . "Two nights gone, and the ground too hard to take her yet. She was good to me." The old woman smooths the sheet. "The custom is simple. You sit with her till dawn. Keep the coins on her eyes. Keep the candle lit. And whatever comes out of the mist tonight - do not let it reach her." Walking back into the hearth room, She lowers herself into the chair by the fire, her eyes moving from the windows, to the hallway, to the door. "There are more ways into this house than there are of you. I know it. So does whatever's waiting out there. I'm too old to hold one anymore, that's your part now." She eyes the part, as if evaluating them each for the task, "Ask me what you need to, while there's quiet left to ask it in." Outside the window, far off in the mist, a dog begins to howl. Then, mid-note, it stops. All eyes are back on Radu.
