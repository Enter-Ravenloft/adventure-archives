DM (Nathan) APP 06-10-2025 06:21 PM
You were taking your ease at the Blood of the Vine, settling in for drinks and conversation (or perhaps brigging) when the door flew open, revealing a villager in a state of agitation. Blood dripped from a long gash across his forehead as he staggered into the bar, stammering about 'Grave-robbing lizard men'. After a bit of effort and a strong drink, he calmed down enough to share that the cemetery at the edge of town has been overrun by lizardfolk who seem to be bent on grave-robbing. The villagers quickly implore the adventurers present to stop the sacrilege, passing a hat to gather coin as a motivation. A small party quickly forms, including yourself. You set out on the misty night, the moist ground helping to disguise your footfalls as you approach the graveyard. Indeed, you quickly come upon a group of the scaled humanoids pulling coffins from the ground and breaking them open. Several corpses have already been piled in a travois tied to a gigantic toad. In addition to the party of grave robbers before you, you hear sounds in the distance suggesting that more such parties are at work - a massive effort to steal fresh corpses for an unknown purpose.
