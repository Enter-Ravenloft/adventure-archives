Cultish Clean-Up On paper, the mission seemed simple. Check out rumours of strange cloaked figures moving about the ancient abandoned temple in the dead of night. If you find out it is indeed some sort of cult trying to use the old demonic summoning circle to tear a hole in the fabric of reality and call forth hellish creatures to plague the citizens of Barovia, stop them before they succeed in that. Straightforward, simple, normal. Easy enough to deal with a handful of zealots with delusions of grandeur. Even easier if they're all already dead, right? Because that's what you find. On arriving at the site of the sightings, a cracked and worn temple facade sunk halfway into the base of the towering mountain behind it, you find no guards, no sentinels, and no visible and audible movement. Instead, you get hit with the stench of blood and rot, rolling like a cloud out of the shattered door. Scattered about, what may have once been people, dressed in what may have once been black robes, but now barely recognisable as either. Only a couple though, as bloody smears seem to indicate the rest of this group has already been dragged further into the old ruin, and likely not in any better condition than the corpses out here. If you're lucky, some wandering monster stumbled across the group and interrupted their ritual before anything could be completed. But since when has anyone in Barovia ever been lucky?
