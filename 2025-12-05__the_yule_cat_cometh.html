The Yule Cat Cometh
Wiz-DM APP 05-12-2025 10:28 PM
Quiet rumors and hushed whispers are shared amongst Barovians and adventures alike. They tell of Jólaköttur, the cat that prowls the snow. Little can be done to satisfy the odd beast who's yowls cannot be deciphered. Few claim to have fled from its dreadful presence. They speak of razor like claws and mind shattering howls. Those that speak of these are the lucky ones. For at least a dozen have been lost to Jólaköttur and frost spirits that accompany. No more! said a few brave souls that have ventured into the snow, seeking to end the triad of the cat. They follow the rumors of wearing old clothing and bathing in herbs to peek the beast's interest. Smelling of catnip and in itchy ill-fitting clothing, four step out of the safety of Vallaiki's walls and into the cold embrace of the Svalich woods.
